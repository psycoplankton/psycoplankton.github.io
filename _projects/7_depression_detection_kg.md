As a **Research Intern at Speech and Language Lab, NTU Singapore**, I am researching synthetic clinical interview generation using LLMs for automated depression screening on the DAIC-WOZ multimodal dataset.

### Methodology & Approach
- Evaluated parameter-efficient fine-tuning (PEFT) techniques including **LoRA** and **LLaMA-Adapter**.
- Designed an augmentation framework using **ECoK Knowledge Graph** (capturing emotional semantics in clinical dialogue) with **KG-BERT** to enrich representations.
- Leveraged **Utterance Emotion Dynamics** ([Daniela et al., 2023](https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.17369)) to trace temporal emotional transitions across interviews.
